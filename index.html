Risk of Prolonged Psychiatric Admission Score (R-PPAS)

Estimates the risk of a prolonged admission (longer than 59 days) for adults in acute psychiatric care, so that discharge planning can begin earlier.

Total score: 0
Low risk

How to read the score. Totals are compared directly against each threshold without rounding: a total of 2.5 falls below the cut-off of 3. Totals are capped at a minimum of 0 and a maximum of 7. Percentages are the observed proportion of admissions in each band that lasted longer than 59 days in the external validation cohort.

What the score does not tell you. The R-PPAS identifies admissions likely to be prolonged. It does not indicate that a longer stay is clinically inappropriate, and a substantial proportion of patients will need more than 59 days. It supports clinical judgement about when to start planning; it does not assess readiness for discharge.
